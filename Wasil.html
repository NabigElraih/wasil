Frequently asked questions

How long does an application take?

It depends on the destination and pathway, from a few weeks for visit visas to many months for residency. We give you a realistic timeline in your written plan.

What does the first consultation cost?

The first call is free and carries no obligation. We assess your options and explain our fees before you decide.

Can you guarantee approval?

No adviser can. Decisions are made by immigration authorities. We make sure your application is complete, accurate and as strong as possible.

Which languages do you support?

We work in Arabic, English and French, by phone, video or message.
